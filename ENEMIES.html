Windbound: enemies

Reference for every enemy in Milestone 1: what it looks like, how it moves, and how the air verbs beat it. The drawings use the game's own placeholder shapes and colors, scaled up 4x.

The impact rule (all enemies)

Enemies have no health. The gust and the dash wake never damage anything, they only push. What a pushed enemy hits decides the outcome, so every result is readable at a glance (GAME_DESIGN.md §7).

Wisp

purple, 10 × 8 px, ground patroller

The basic ground enemy. It walks back and forth on its platform and never steps off a ledge by itself, so its route is easy to read. A gust knocks it back about 40 px with a small hop and leaves it dizzy for a second. While it's dizzy it can't hurt you and it doesn't avoid ledges, which opens three ways to beat it: knock it into spikes or off into a pit, slam it into a wall for a longer 2 s stun and then dash through it, or hit it with a reflected sentry orb. Dashing into a walking wisp still kills you, so stun first.

  • Movement use: a dash kill refills the dash and down-boosts, so a wisp works as a mid-air recharge, like a wind crystal.
  • Puzzle use: a moving hazard that guards a platform until you deal with it.
  • Tunables (Inspector, per wisp): start_direction, patrol_speed, stun_time, wall_slam_stun_time, wall_slam_min_speed, knockback_hop_speed, friction and gravity.

Sentry

purple, 12 × 12 px, stationary turret

A turret that controls space. It only wakes up while you're in its room, within 220 px, and in clear line of sight. Walls, crates and doors all block its view, so cover is always a choice. The first shot comes a second after it spots you, and every shot is telegraphed, so its rhythm is learnable. It can't be pushed. The only way to destroy it is to send its own orb back with a gust. Ceiling-mounted sentries watch the floor below; a small platform overhead becomes a hiding spot.

  • Combat use: reflect its orb back at it, or at other enemies.
  • Puzzle use: a timing gate. Cross its field of view between shots, or use cover.
  • Tunables: fire_interval, first_shot_delay, telegraph_time, sight_range, orb_speed, mounted_on_ceiling, can_fire.

Sentry orb

orange, radius 3 (hitbox 2), projectile

A slow shot (60 px/s), aimed where you were when it fired. It pops on anything solid and flies past wisps. Hit it with a gust or the dash wake and it's reflected: it turns pale gold, speeds up to 90 px/s, can no longer hurt you, and destroys any sentry or wisp it touches. Aim assist: 8-direction aiming is coarse, so among the enemies in the same room that are within 45° of your gust's direction and in clear view, the orb picks the one closest to that direction and flies straight at it. With nothing in that cone, it simply follows the gust. A target sitting on top of a platform is shielded from below, so get to its height first.

  • Tunables (on the orb scene): reflected_speed_multiplier, reflect_assist_degrees, max_lifetime.

Code: scenes/enemies/sentry_orb.gd · Never placed by hand: sentries spawn it.

Adding a new enemy

  • Body on layer 5 ("enemies"); add layer 4 ("pushable") and the pushable group plus receive_gust() if the gust should move it.
  • Join Room.RESETTABLE_GROUP and implement reset(), so dying or pressing R brings it back.
  • Join SentryOrb.TARGET_GROUP with get_orb_target_position() and is_valid_orb_target() to make it a reflection target.
  • Follow the impact rule: no HP, outcomes come from what it's pushed into.
  • Add a card to this page: drawing, one paragraph, tunables.
