Who beats whom

Enemies have no health. Every hit goes through one path (EnemyHit) and each enemy's table (ElementImmunity) says what that kind of hit does to it: kill, push, stagger, reflect or nothing. Each enemy is a wall for some elements and an opening for others (Fire_bending.md §7.2). Pushes follow the impact rule: what the enemy is pushed into decides the outcome (GAME_DESIGN.md §7).

Wisp

Air purple, 10 × 8 px, ground patroller

The basic ground enemy. It walks back and forth on its platform and never steps off a ledge by itself, so its route is easy to read. A gust knocks it back about 40 px with a small hop and leaves it dizzy for a second and a half: harmless, and blind to ledges. Then knock it into spikes or a pit, or slam it into a wall for a longer 2.5 s stun. Every other element beats it directly: fire burns it up, a big or perfect rock and a reflected orb defeat it, a column or the snake throws it.

  • Puzzle use: a moving hazard that guards a platform until you deal with it. It doesn't avoid fire, so a grass fuse can be timed to meet its patrol.
  • Tunables (Inspector, per wisp): start_direction, patrol_speed, stun_time, wall_slam_stun_time, wall_slam_min_speed, knockback_hop_speed, friction and gravity.

Leaf wisp

Fire leaf greens, purple eyes, 10 × 8 px, ground patroller

A wisp made of leaves. It patrols exactly like the wisp (walls and ledges turn it, touching it kills), but it can't be blown around or stunned, so it never becomes harmless. Rocks pass through the leaves, columns and the snake don't move it, it walks over spikes, and reflected orbs ignore it. The only answer is fire: a fireball, the jet, a grass fuse timed to its patrol, or a torch-monkey running into it. In a room with no pips to spare, it asks where the fire will come from.

  • Puzzle use: the fire-only gatekeeper. Pair it with a grass fuse or a monkey to make lighting the right thing at the right time the puzzle.
  • Tunables (Inspector, per leaf wisp): start_direction, patrol_speed, gravity. The look's spring and gust ruffle are constants in the script.

Sentry

Air purple (ember orange with fire orbs), 12 × 12 px, stationary turret

A turret that controls space. It only counts down while you're in its room, within 220 px, and in clear line of sight: walls, crates and doors block its view, so cover is always a choice. The first shot comes a second after it spots you, and every shot is telegraphed, so its rhythm is learnable. It can't be pushed and you can stand on it. Send its own orb back with a gust, throw a big or perfect rock, or hit it with a fireball. It's metal, so flames and burning grass do nothing. With fire_orbs it throws your own fireball instead (same arc, bounces and lighting), made hostile; gusts don't reflect a fireball.

  • Puzzle use: a timing gate. Cross its field of view between shots, or use cover. A fire sentry lights grass for you, when you want it to or not.
  • Tunables: fire_interval, first_shot_delay, telegraph_time, sight_range, orb_speed, mounted_on_ceiling, can_fire, fire_orbs.

Sentry orb

Air orange, radius 3 (hitbox 2), projectile

A slow shot (60 px/s), aimed where you were when it fired. Since Stage F12 it bounces off walls, floors, crates and doors (up to max_bounces, then it pops), and off the Shell's plates. Hit it with a gust and it's reflected: pale gold, 90 px/s, harmless to you, and it destroys any sentry or wisp it touches. Aim assist: among the enemies in the room within 45° of your gust and in clear view, it flies straight at the closest-angle one; otherwise it follows the gust. A fireball meeting it head-on cancels both. Any water soaks it: it slows, falls, and pops on the next solid.

  • Tunables (on the orb scene): reflected_speed_multiplier, reflect_assist_degrees, max_bounces, max_lifetime. Water: WaterTuning, "Water and projectiles".

Vine monkey

Fire thicket greens, dark outline, purple eye; small and light

The agile leafling: a ball of thicket with vine arms and a curled tail. It runs a readable loop, hopping gaps it can make and turning at walls. Only fire kills it, and it doesn't die at once: lit, it panics for a few seconds as a running torch that lights grass, thicket, leaf piles, vines, air diamonds and wisps, and kills on touch, before it crumbles to ash. That makes it a moving fuse you aim. A gust snuffs its fire (it smolders, stunned, and can be lit again). It's light: gusts blow it far, hot updrafts lift it, wind carries it. Rocks, columns and the snake only push it, and spikes don't hurt it.

  • Puzzle use: carry fire across a gap. Light it mid-hop and it lands on the far rim, still burning.
  • Tunables: FireTuning, group "Vine monkey" (run and torch speed, hop reach, torch time, stun times). Per monkey: start_direction, hops_gaps.

Thicket elephant

Fire stone with moss, a thicket trunk and a vine tail, 96 × 60 px

The heavy leafling: a slow, blocky heap of boulders with three burnable spots. Burn all three and it crumbles into a big heap of ash. Its front (head, tusks, trunk) kills on touch, but the rest is safe: its back is a moving platform you can ride over spikes or up to high diamonds, and you can stand under its belly. When it can hit you (in front, on its level, in clear view) it rears up, the tell, then charges, smashing cracked walls, thicket and stone walls, which only it can break. Burns set it off too: a burnt trunk makes it charge forward, a burnt tail makes it bolt away from the fire. Every few seconds its tail wiggles, rises and whips down behind it, killing whatever it hits. A big rock pushes it back and stops a charge, a gust barely nudges it, small rocks bounce off, and columns and the snake break on it.

  • Puzzle use: a ram you aim at walls by standing where you want it to charge, and a ride you have to burn your way off.
  • Tunables: FireTuning, group "Thicket elephant" (walk and charge speed, rear time, whip timing, spot burn time, pushes).

Shell

Earth slate plates, ochre seams, 12 × 8 px, armored crawler

Earth's armored crawler, built in the Fire chapter as the obstacle fire can't touch. It patrols slowly, turning at walls, ledges and spikes, and it always kills on touch, even staggered. Only a big rock cracks it open: a thrown 16 × 16 rock, or a loose big rock dropped on it from two tiles up. Or crush it: ram it into a wall with the snake's head, hit it with a small rock when a wall is a tile behind it, or press it against a ceiling with a rising column. Everything else is shrugged off: fireballs and orbs bounce, flames and burning grass do nothing, a small rock only staggers it, a gust barely nudges it. When the floor under it goes (a thicket floor burns away) it falls, and walks on wherever it lands.

  • Puzzle use: the reason to carry Earth into a Fire room, and a fireball ricochet surface.
  • Tunables: FireTuning, group "Shell" (walk speed, stagger time and knockback, crush reach, gust nudge).

Adding a new enemy

  • Body on layer 5 ("enemies"); add layer 4 ("pushable") and the pushable group plus receive_gust() if the gust should reach it.
  • Join EnemyHit.GROUP with receive_hit(), and give it an ElementImmunity table: every hit (fire, rocks, gusts, spikes, orbs, crushing, the whip) goes through it.
  • Join Flammable.GROUP (ignite, is_burning, snuff, get_fire_rect) if flames should reach it.
  • Join Room.RESETTABLE_GROUP and implement reset(), so dying or pressing R brings it back, and emit defeated so enemy gates can count it.
  • Join SentryOrb.TARGET_GROUP with get_orb_target_position() and is_valid_orb_target() to make it a reflection target.
  • Add its palette entry and icon (palette_catalog.gd, palette_icons.gd), its row in the table above, and a card on this page.
