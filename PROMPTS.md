## Setup (once, by hand)

1. Create an empty folder, e.g. `windbound/`, and put `CLAUDE.md`, `GAME_DESIGN.md`, `PROMPTS.md` in it, plus `stage.md` in `.claude/commands/` (so the path is `windbound/.claude/commands/stage.md`).
2. Open a terminal in the folder and run `claude`.
3. Type `/stage 0`. Claude Code handles the rest of the setup, including the Godot MCP.

## Stage W8 — The wet layer (W8a, W8b, W8c)

## Stage W8a — Wet tiles and mud
> A `SurfaceGrid` (scenes/objects/water/surface_grid.gd, made by the WaterWorld like the PlayerIceLayer; the wet half of what `FireGrid` is for fire) keeps one wet state per tile, for the room's terrain tiles and the FireGrid's cover cells. It takes over the W2 soak stub (`WaterWorld.soaked_cells` / `is_wet`): the books stay as they are. WHAT GETS WET: **grey ground** ("Tiles") turns WET_ROCK as soon as any water touches one of its faces (a body's floor or wall, a particle hitting it, a snake point). It soaks nothing, and water runs and puddles on it as today. **Earth** (fresh / used / hardened) and **ash** (a FireGrid ASH cell) are absorbent (`soak_per_tile`) and turn into MUD once full. Earth mud keeps its fresh/used state underneath. Ash mud is the same mud with a darker look, and it never sets into ashstone. **Grass and thicket** are absorbent and turn WET_GRASS / WET_THICKET: they won't burn while wet. Growing tall grass and branches is W9; leave a `grow` hook. MUD PHYSICS (all in `WaterTuning`): run ×`mud_run_multiplier` (0.5), jump ×`mud_jump_multiplier` (0.65); landing on mud cuts every bit of horizontal speed at once (the brake at the end of an ice runway); on a mud wall, holding toward it CLINGS: no wall slide and no climb, and wall jumps still work. Wet grey ground and wet grass play like dry ground. HEAT DRIES: a DIRECT flame (`Flammable.ignite_at` from a fireball, the flame jet's plume, a bonfire) on a wet tile dries it in a steam puff instead of igniting it, and the next flame ignites as usual. Fire SPREADING from a neighbour doesn't dry a wet tile, so a wet line is a firebreak. `Flammable.douse_near` now also wets what it douses. A `Wettable` contract (like `Flammable`): optional `wet()` / `is_wet()` on objects, plus `Wettable.wet_near(point, radius)` that the WaterWorld calls where water lands. No object implements it yet. LOOKS: wet grey darker with a sheen line on top; mud glossy brown with slow drips (ash mud grey-black); wet grass a deeper green with a few drops; hand prints where you cling. Everything wet resets with the room. Room Water J (`build_water_rooms -- water_j`): an ice trail ending on a mud floor, a tall earth shaft with no ledges and a pool at its foot, a strip of grass with a burning bonfire past it, and grey and ash floors to wet. ## Tips for vibe coding with Claude Code
- **Describe feel, not code:** "the jump feels floaty at the top" or "the dash stops too abruptly" works better than guessing which variable to change.
- **Report what you see:** copy error text from Godot's Output/Debugger panel into Claude Code.
- **Commit after each stage** so you can roll back if a change breaks things.
- **Use `/clear` between stages** to keep context focused; CLAUDE.md is reloaded automatically.
- When a stage gets big, ask Claude Code to make a plan first and approve it before it writes code.
